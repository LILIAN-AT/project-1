About IUIU

The Islamic University In Uganda (IUIU) is a private university that offers a wide range of academic programs in various fields of study.

Islamic University In Uganda (IUIU) is among the top 20 leading universities in Uganda and East Africa at large.

It was established in 2007 and has since grown to become a prominent institution for higher education in the region.
